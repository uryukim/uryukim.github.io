I started roleplaying when i was 15 years old. I saw my friend with a
different facebook account and I asked her, "Who's that?", and that 
person was my 4-year crush since High School. 

## It started with questions

"Won't facebook block you for that?" - I said it to her calmly because
she is not aware of my feelings for her. She quietly said "There are a 
million roleplayers and most of them are filipinos, I don't know where
this started, but I enjoy meeting a lot of random people even if I don't
know their real names and faces."

I created an account hours after that conversation with her, hoping 
that I can find her, I didn't know her rp name and I forgot about it as I 
wandered through the environment where different kinds of people are
enjoying with posting, commenting, and engaging with this community.

## It is an environment that you can leave

Months have passed and an important relative of mine passed away, I quit 
all of my social medias and went on with months of grieve. I enjoyed 
designing things such as publications, logos, anything there is to design
as long as my laptop can handle it.

## On and Off, and On

I then went back to the roleplaying world, eager to talk with people,
release anything that I want to release, comment, post, rant, and doing
something that releases my burdens. I wish I had went back sooner.
