Every great design starts with a mess. Seriously. My desk, my notes, my
Figma files — they all start as beautiful chaos before they become
something cohesive.

## It Starts with Questions

Before I open any design tool, I ask questions. *A lot* of them. What
problem are we solving? Who are we talking to? What should they feel when
they see this? The answers shape everything that follows.

## Mood Boarding & Exploration

I collect references obsessively — not just from design, but from
architecture, film, nature, even food. The best visual solutions often
come from unexpected places.

## Iteration, Iteration, Iteration

I believe in quantity before quality. First drafts are supposed to be
rough. I explore wildly, then refine ruthlessly. By the third or fourth
round, something magical usually emerges.

## The Final Polish

This is where the engineering side of me kicks in. Every pixel gets
attention. Spacing, alignment, typography — the details that most people
don't consciously notice but absolutely *feel*.

---

Design is a conversation between intention and intuition. The best work
happens when you trust both.
